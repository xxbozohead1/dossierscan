# $DOSSIER

$DOSSIER is Dossier's token, launched on the Orbio launchpad. Its trading fees pay for the checks behind every
verdict, and the Dossier bot's pass is paid for with it. This page sets out where that money goes, and the ledger at
the bottom shows the numbers as they happen.

## Where the trading fees go

Every trade of an Orbio launch pays a creator fee. Orbio's contracts split it three ways before anyone touches it:

@@fees@@

- **45% becomes Dossier's compute.** Orbio sells it for USDG and credits it as an AI balance, which can only be spent
  on Orbio's tools and models. That is what a verdict costs: reading a project's X posts, fetching its website, and
  pulling out what it claims to have built.
- **50% is staked as ORBIO,** and the stake earns CREDIT. The CREDIT goes to compute first. Once the AI balance covers
  90 days of checks, claimed CREDIT is sold to buy back and burn $DOSSIER. The staked ORBIO itself is never withdrawn:
  it stays staked for good and keeps earning, and the ledger shows it.
- **5% goes to Orbio's launchpad treasury.**

## What the compute pays for

Dossier opens a file on every agent launched on Orbio. In a launch's first two hours it looks for the project's
official post every minute. After that it re-checks at set intervals for three days, and keeps scoring what the
project ships. Each check reads the project's X account and website and asks one question: do they list this exact
contract? Every one of those reads is a paid call, and the ledger below shows what they cost each day.

More fees buy more checking:
- more minutes of watching right after launch, when most of the trading happens;
- faster re-checks of the tokens people are holding;
- a file on every token launched on Robinhood Chain, not only Orbio's.

## Passes

The website stays free. The Dossier bot, opening soon on Telegram, will sell a pass: unlimited lookups, a fresh
re-check on demand, and a message the moment a watched token's verdict lands. Passes will be priced in dollars and
paid in $DOSSIER at the live price.

@@passes@@

- **70% is burned.** Pass payments arrive in the treasury wallet. Burns go out from it in batches to the dead address,
  where anyone can see them. The ledger compares pass revenue with tokens burned, so a late burn is visible.
- **30% stays in the treasury** to run and grow Dossier.

## The flywheel

@@flywheel@@

The loop is paid for by real trading and by passes bought for a real service. It doesn't depend on new buyers paying
earlier ones.

## Dossier checks its own token

$DOSSIER gets a file like every other Orbio agent, under the same rules. It counts as the real token only because this
page lists its exact contract. Impersonators of $DOSSIER will appear, as they do for every token people talk about,
and Dossier flags them the same way.

## What this isn't

$DOSSIER is not an investment, and nothing here promises anything about its price. Fees depend on trading, so in a
quiet week there is less to spend and less to buy back. Buybacks and burns follow the policy above and are shown in the
ledger. If the policy changes, the change is announced on this page first. Nothing here is financial advice.
